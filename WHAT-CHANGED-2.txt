"ALL YOUR MONEY BACK" — consistency pass, 08/10/2026
Three files replaced: index.html, terms-conditions.html
(disclaimer.html unchanged from the last batch — it never mentioned money)

WHY
"Service fee" was jargon. Since your clients put their own card on the ad
account, every dollar they pay YOU is the fee — so "all your money back" is
literally true, not a softer version of the promise. The site now says that
plainly and the Terms say the same thing in the same words.

HOME PAGE (index.html)
Headline:
  before  30 qualified buyer leads in 90 days. Or every dollar back.
  after   30 qualified buyer leads in 90 days. Or all your money back.

Body:
  before  ...you get 100% of your service fee back — and you keep the entire
          system we built.
  after   ...you get all your money back — every dollar you've paid us — and you
          keep the entire system we built.

Fine print:
  before  Requires maintaining your agreed minimum ad spend.
  after   Requires maintaining your agreed minimum ad spend, billed by the ad
          platform directly to your own card — that budget never passes through us.
  (This is what makes "all your money back" airtight: it says up front that the
  ad budget was never yours to refund, so nobody can claim they expected it back.)

TERMS & CONDITIONS — Section 3.1
  before  ...you receive a full refund of the service fees paid for that period
  after   ...you get all your money back — a full refund of every dollar you have
          paid us for that period
  before  Advertising spend is paid directly to the advertising platform and is
          not refundable by us.
  after   Advertising budget is billed by the advertising platform directly to you
          and is never paid to us, so it is not part of any refund.

TERMS & CONDITIONS — Section 5
  before  Service fees are non-refundable except where a refund is due under the
          performance guarantee in Section 3.1
  after   Fees are non-refundable except where a refund is due under the
          performance guarantee in Section 3.1, which refunds everything you have
          paid us for the guarantee period

Section 10's liability carve-out from the last batch still stands and needs no
change — it already says "except for a refund owed under Section 3.1."

CHECKED
- "service fee" no longer appears anywhere on the site
- Nothing else on the site mentions the refund, so there is no fourth place to fix
- The guarantee section still fits one full screen with no cut-off at 1440x900,
  1280x720 and 390x844 (the longer paragraph did not push anything off-screen)
- No horizontal overflow on either page, desktop or phone

ONE THING THIS DEPENDS ON
Your signed service agreement has to say the same thing. Section 3.1 states the
signed agreement governs if the two differ — so if the agreement still says
"service fees," that is the number a client would actually be owed. Get the two
matched, and have a lawyer look at the pair.

day 40. The documents are silent on it.
