WHAT CHANGED — legal pages, 08/09/2026
Two files replaced: terms-conditions.html, disclaimer.html

THE PROBLEM
The site advertises "30 qualified buyer leads in 90 days. Or every dollar back."
The Terms said "No Guarantees" and listed "Lead volume" as something not
guaranteed. The Disclaimer said "We do not guarantee: A specific number of
leads." Three places contradicted the refund promise, plus a fourth: the
liability cap in Terms Section 10 capped everything at 30 days of fees, while
the guarantee refunds 90 days.

THE APPROACH
Rather than delete the disclaimers (which protect you on everything else), the
guarantee is now stated affirmatively inside the Terms as Section 3.1, and every
disclaimer carves it out by name. The documents now agree instead of one
cancelling the other.

TERMS & CONDITIONS
1. Heading: "3. No Guarantees" -> "3. No Guarantees (One Exception)"
2. Intro now begins "Except for the written performance guarantee described in
   Section 3.1 below..."
3. Bullet "Lead volume" -> "Lead volume beyond any guaranteed minimum"
4. Added bullet "Closed transactions" (you disclaim closings on the site)
5. NEW Section 3.1 Performance Guarantee — states the 30 leads / 90 days /
   full service-fee refund promise, defines what counts as a qualified lead
   (5 criteria), and states the conditions: buyer campaigns only, agreed minimum
   ad budget maintained for the full 90 days, ad spend paid to the platform and
   not refundable by us, lead volume only (not appointments/closings/revenue).
   Ends: this is the only performance guarantee, and the signed service
   agreement governs if it differs.
6. Section 5: "Fees are non-refundable unless otherwise agreed in writing" ->
   "Service fees are non-refundable except where a refund is due under the
   performance guarantee in Section 3.1"
7. Section 10: liability cap now begins "Except for a refund owed under the
   performance guarantee in Section 3.1..."
8. Effective Date 03/24/2026 -> 08/09/2026

DISCLAIMER
1. The "illustrative purposes only" paragraph now ends: "This does not limit the
   written performance guarantee described in Section 3.1 of our Terms &
   Conditions and in your signed service agreement." (links to the Terms page)
2. "We do not guarantee:" -> "Except for that written performance guarantee, we
   do not guarantee:"
3. Bullet "A specific number of leads" -> "Lead volume beyond any guaranteed
   minimum"
Everything else on both pages is untouched.

HOW TO PUBLISH
GitHub repo -> click terms-conditions.html -> pencil icon -> select all, paste
the new version -> Commit changes. Repeat for disclaimer.html. Cloudflare
redeploys in about a minute. Or drag both files into the repo's "Add file ->
Upload files" and let them overwrite.

TWO THINGS TO DECIDE
1. NOT A LAWYER. I am not one, and this is a written refund obligation. Have a
   lawyer read Section 3.1 before it goes live, and make sure the wording in
   your signed service agreement matches it. Section 3.1 says the signed
   agreement wins if the two differ — so the agreement is the document that
   actually binds you.
2. "Or every dollar back" on the homepage. Your own fine print says the refund
   is your service fee, and ad spend goes to Meta and does not come back. The
   headline promises more than the terms deliver. Changing it to "Or every
   dollar of our fee back" closes that gap without weakening the offer. Your
   call — say the word and I will make the edit.
3. Termination gap: the Terms do not say what happens to the guarantee if a
   client cancels at day 40. Worth deciding with your lawyer whether early
   termination voids it.
