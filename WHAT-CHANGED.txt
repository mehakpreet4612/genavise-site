FIRST: A GAP WORTH KNOWING ABOUT
--------------------------------
Your new site had NO thank-you page. Only the Framer one has it. If your
GoHighLevel calendar is set to redirect to /thank-you after someone books, they
were landing on your 404 page - right at the moment they've just committed.
This page fixes that.

After uploading, check the redirect URL in GoHighLevel:
  Calendar settings -> Confirmation / After booking -> Redirect URL
  Set it to:  https://genavise.com/thank-you
  (or https://genavise-site.pages.dev/thank-you while you're still testing)


ON THE HANDWRITING - MY HONEST TAKE
-----------------------------------
The instinct is right. A warm, handwritten confirmation page is genuinely good,
and post-booking is exactly the moment to be human. Keep the idea.

The problem is that the Excalidraw version is a PICTURE of writing, not writing.
That costs you five things:

1. MOBILE. This is the big one. A wide image of handwriting scaled down to a
   390px phone screen either shrinks until it's unreadable or forces sideways
   scrolling. Most people book on their phone.
2. It can't be edited. Change one word and you're back in Excalidraw
   re-exporting and re-uploading.
3. Screen readers and search engines read nothing at all - it's just pixels.
4. It renders soft on high-resolution screens unless exported at 2x or more.
5. Nobody can select or copy the text - which matters here, because the page
   is giving people instructions.

There's also a typo baked into the image: "4 confirmation with your date..."
The handwritten A reads as a 4. You can't fix that without re-exporting.

THE CLEANER WAY (what this page does)
-------------------------------------
Real HTML text set in a real handwriting font, self-hosted so it loads fast and
doesn't depend on Google. You keep the exact handwritten feel and lose every one
of those five problems: it reflows on a phone, you edit it by changing a word in
the HTML, screen readers read it, it's razor sharp at any resolution, and people
can copy it.

The arrow stays a drawing - as an inline SVG, because an arrow genuinely IS a
drawing, not text. Same for your signature.

The font is Architects Daughter, the closest widely-available match to
Excalidraw's own handwriting. If you'd prefer something more looping and
signature-like, say so and I'll swap it for Caveat - it's a one-line change.

I also set this page to noindex, and kept it out of your sitemap. Confirmation
pages shouldn't turn up in Google search results.

YOUR SIGNATURE
--------------
There's a slot for it. Export your signature from Excalidraw as a PNG with a
transparent background, name it signature.png, and upload it alongside these
files. It'll appear under "See you on the call." If you don't upload one, the
slot vanishes cleanly - no empty box.
