THE FOUR FIXES
--------------

1. MORE AIR ABOVE THE NAV
   The logo sat 20px below the top of the page, which is why it felt cramped
   against the browser's own toolbar. It now sits at 74px, and the logo, nav
   links and button are all vertically centred with each other. That matches the
   proportions of your Framer version.

2. EVERY SCREENSHOT IS NOW FRAMED
   All four images get a 1px #e0e0e0 border with an 8px white inset, and the
   image inside gets slightly rounded corners. Your screenshots are mostly
   white, so on a white page they were bleeding into the background and reading
   as random floating fragments. The frame gives them an edge.

3. THE GAP BETWEEN THE FOUR ICONS AND THE BUTTON
   This was a real bug, not a spacing choice. The rule that centres a section's
   content used automatic margins, and with two blocks on one screen - the icon
   row and the button - those margins shoved them to opposite ends, opening
   about 300px of dead space. Removed; the pair is now centred as a group with a
   deliberate 52px gap.

4. THE BOOKING CALENDAR IS NARROWER AND HIGHER
   It was running the full 1680px width, so on a laptop you got a slab you had
   to scroll around. Now capped at 1080px and centred - GoHighLevel keeps its
   two-column layout down to about 900px, so nothing is lost and it reads as a
   focused card. I also cut the space above it, pulling the calendar roughly
   90px further up the page so more of it is visible without scrolling.

   Note: I do not set a height on that iframe. GoHighLevel's own script measures
   its content and sets the height itself - I tried overriding it while testing
   and the whole widget collapsed to a sliver. The min-height in the CSS is only
   a floor so the page does not jump while the calendar loads.
