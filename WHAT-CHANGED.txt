WHAT I CHANGED
--------------

1. ONE SECTION PER SCREEN
   Every major block on the home page now sits in a wrapper that fills the
   viewport and centres its content. Scrolling settles on one section at a
   time - no more Step 2 peeking above Step 3, no FAQ title creeping in at
   the bottom of the guarantee.

   The old version used fixed pixel padding, which is why it drifted: 376px
   of space looks right on one screen size and wrong on every other. This
   version measures against the actual window height, so it holds up on a
   laptop, a big monitor and a phone alike.

2. THE STICKY HEADER NO LONGER SWALLOWS HEADINGS
   The header sits on top of the page, so centred content could slide
   underneath it - that's why the "FAQs" title vanished when you clicked FAQs
   in the nav. Each screen now reserves the header's height inside itself, so
   content centres in the space you can actually see.

3. THE NAV LINKS LAND CENTRED
   Results, How It Works and FAQs previously used fixed pixel offsets that
   pushed content to the bottom of the screen. Those are gone - the centring
   does the positioning now, so every jump lands mid-screen with the section
   heading visible.

4. MID-PAGE CTA MOVED
   The lone "Book A Strategy Call" button between Step 3 and the four icons
   would have needed a whole empty screen to itself. It now sits under the
   four icons on the same screen, which reads better and saves a dead scroll.

5. BOOKING CALENDAR
   Your GoHighLevel calendar is embedded on booking.html, full width with a
   798px floor so the page doesn't jump while it loads. GoHighLevel's own
   script sets the final height once the calendar appears.
