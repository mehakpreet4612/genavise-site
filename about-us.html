ABOUT US

We build systems. Not just campaigns.

Genavise Marketing was built around a simple idea: most real estate agents don't have a customer problem — they have a system problem.

Too many agents are stuck in feast-or-famine cycles. One month is busy. The next is slow. New contacts slip through the cracks, follow-up depends on memory, and marketing feels like guesswork. We exist to fix that.

What we actually do

We help real estate agents generate qualified buyer leads, and the first conversation with each one, through structured Facebook advertising run on the agent's own ad account and automated follow-up sent in the agent's name.

That means targeted ad campaigns, instant SMS and email follow-up, built-in qualification, automated booking logic, and clear pipeline tracking. Not just ads. The full machine.

Who we work with

We work with serious real estate agents who want structure, consistency, and scalable growth. If you're looking for overnight magic, this isn't for you. If you're ready to install a system you own that produces qualified buyer conversations month after month, we should talk.

Our philosophy

Speed wins attention. Structure wins consistency. Systems win long-term.
