CASE STUDY · MARYLAND

33 buyer leads. $17.10 each. Every one called within 5 minutes.

A Maryland residential agent came to us with leads from referrals, Zillow, and a paid agency behind him. The pattern was the same across all of it: call the number, nobody answers — and when somebody did, it was usually a renter. He didn't need more leads. He needed control over who came in and what happened in the first five minutes after they did.

Between April 21 and May 30, the campaign generated 33 buyer leads on $564.30 in ad spend — $17.10 per lead, against a typical range of $25–50. That number came from testing: 14 ad variants ran, one won at $15.06 per lead, and roughly 85% of the budget was concentrated behind it once it proved out.

The number that mattered most: 65–70% of leads answered the automated 5-minute callback. A person called within five minutes still has their phone in hand and remembers raising it. A person called hours later is a cold call with extra steps. Of the leads who answered, a meaningful share booked a meeting with the agent — we only publish figures we can back with a screenshot.
